# Radiochromic Film Analyzer

Desktop software for radiochromic-film image analysis, scanner flat-field correction, dose-response calibration, uncertainty propagation and traceable result export.

## Scope

The software preserves acquisition values, calibration provenance and uncertainty information. Metrological validity remains specific to the scanner, film lot, irradiation protocol and reference-dose system used for a measurement campaign.
